Signing up the long tail of small ISPs

Sofia Iuteri, Washington and Lee University · November 2026

Thousands of small ISPs can't reach every home they're asked to serve. They're too small for a reseller program and too many to sign by hand. The fix: an offer built for them, and agents that do the research, outreach and onboarding, tested first in a Virginia pilot.

Why it's been hard

Four likely reasons, all fixable

01

Built for bigger partners

Reported reseller terms expected millions in annual revenue. An 800-customer ISP can't meet that.

02

Sounds like "help your competitor"

Many small ISPs lose customers to Starlink. A reseller pitch gets ignored.

03

The math doesn't work by hand

One sales call per tiny ISP costs more than the few dozen subscribers it brings.

04

Generic outreach

Local owners answer emails that show you know their county, not templates.
