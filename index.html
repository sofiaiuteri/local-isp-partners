A concept for Starlink · Virginia

Every small ISP has a list of homes it can't reach.

The ridges in Virginia block radio towers, and long rural roads make fiber too expensive. About 2,500 small providers across the US face some version of this. This is a proposal for turning those homes into Starlink customers through the local ISP, starting with 17 providers in Virginia.

Sperryville, Rappahannock County

The one that's already doing it

Piedmont Broadband has served Rappahannock County since 2008 from more than 100 wireless access points. Its website is upfront about the limits. The equipment needs line of sight, and in its words, "the terrain out here doesn't always cooperate."

So for homes it can't reach, Piedmont installs Starlink, $250 standard. That works for the customer. But Piedmont is paid once, and every month after that goes straight to Starlink.

A partner program would keep Piedmont in that relationship and give it a reason to do the same for every home on its list. There are businesses like this in almost every rural county.
