How it works

You already know which homes you can't get to. This program gives you something to offer them besides "not yet."

01

Offer

When an address is out of range for your towers or fiber, quote Starlink under your name. It also works as a backup line for business customers.

02

Install

Your crew does the install and gets paid for it. We ship kits to you or straight to the customer, whichever you prefer.

03

Earn

You earn a monthly share for every active subscriber you bring on. If you extend your network to them later, you can move them over.

Straight answers

These are the questions local providers ask first. The answers describe the program as proposed, and Starlink would set the final terms.

Isn't Starlink my competitor?

For the homes you already serve, nothing changes. This program only covers addresses you can't reach and backup lines for your business customers. Those people are going to look at Starlink either way. As a partner, they come through you, stay your customers, and you get paid.

What does it cost to join?

The proposal is no fee, no inventory to buy up front, and no volume minimum, so a provider could sign up one customer a year or a hundred. That's the main change from a traditional reseller program, which is built for much larger partners.

Who handles installs and support?

Your crew handles installs and first-line support, the same way you do today. Starlink handles the network, hardware warranty, and replacements. The proposal includes a dedicated partner support line, so you don't wait in the consumer queue.

Can I still build out to these homes later?

Yes. If your network reaches them later, you can move them onto your own service. Nothing locks them into Starlink.

How does this fit with my BEAD or VATI build?

Use it for the addresses outside your funded build area, or to get people online while construction is underway. Check your grant terms for anything inside a funded project area.

How long does sign-up take?

The goal is about 15 minutes online, then a short training session and a demo kit for your crew to practice on.

Proposed partner agreement, short version
  • You offer Starlink to addresses outside your network and to business customers as a backup line.
  • You earn an install fee you set and a monthly share for each active subscriber.
  • No fees, minimums, or exclusivity. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice.
  • You keep the customer relationship and handle first-line support.

You're in

Here's what happens next.

  1. Your signed agreement is saved in your partner portal.
  2. Book a 30-minute training on quoting, installs and support handoffs.
  3. Confirm where to ship your demo kit, so your crew can do a practice install.
  4. From then on, quote Starlink for any out-of-range address and track your earnings in the portal.
