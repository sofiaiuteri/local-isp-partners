Straight answers

What local providers ask first. Starlink would set final terms.

Isn't Starlink my competitor?

Not for homes you already serve. This only covers addresses you can't reach and business backup lines. Those people will look at Starlink anyway; this way they stay your customers and you get paid.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. No fee, no upfront inventory and no minimum, so one customer a year is fine.

Who handles installs and support?

Your crew does installs and first-line support. Starlink covers the network, warranty and replacements, with a dedicated partner support line.

Can I still build out to these homes later?

Yes. Move them onto your network whenever it reaches them.

How does this fit with my BEAD or VATI build?

Use it outside your funded area, or to get people online during construction. Check your grant terms inside it.

How long does sign-up take?

About 15 minutes, then a short training and a demo kit for your crew.

Proposed partner agreement, short version
  • You offer Starlink to addresses outside your network and to business customers as a backup line.
  • You earn an install fee you set and a monthly share for each active subscriber.
  • No fees, minimums, or exclusivity. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice.
  • You keep the customer relationship and handle first-line support.

You're in

Here's what happens next.

  1. Your signed agreement is saved in your partner portal.
  2. Book a 30-minute training on quoting, installs and support handoffs.
  3. Confirm where to ship your demo kit, so your crew can do a practice install.
  4. From then on, quote Starlink for any out-of-range address and track your earnings in the portal.
